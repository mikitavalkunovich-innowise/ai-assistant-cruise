# NCL Benefits Overview

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Applies to:** Full-time employees (ship, hotel, shore)

## Health & Wellness

### Medical Coverage
- **Ship/Hotel Staff:** Shipboard medical clinic access; emergency evacuation insurance included
- **Shore Staff:** Choice of 2 medical plans (PPO or HDHP) — company covers 80% of premium
- **Dental & Vision:** Available for shore staff; ship staff receive basic dental at clinic

### Mental Health
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP): free confidential counseling, 6 sessions per year
- Headspace app subscription: free for all employees

## Financial Benefits

### Retirement
- **401(k):** Shore staff — company match up to 4% after 90 days
- **MLC Pension:** Seafarers — per flag state and union agreement requirements

### Employee Discounts
- 50% discount on NCL cruises for employee + 3 guests (subject to availability)
- Partner discounts: hotels, rental cars, retail (see Crew Portal → Benefits)

## Work-Life Benefits

- **Uniform allowance:** Provided per department standards
- **Laundry service:** Complimentary for ship-based crew
- **Meals:** Complimentary crew mess on board; meal allowance for hotel staff
- **Internet:** Subsidized crew internet packages on board

## Leave Benefits

See Leave Policy for annual leave entitlements. Additional benefits:
- Paid holidays per contract type
- Sabbatical eligibility after 10 years (shore staff, manager approval)

## Enrollment

New hires: complete benefits enrollment within **30 days** of start date via Crew Portal → Benefits → Enroll.

Questions: benefits@ncl.com
