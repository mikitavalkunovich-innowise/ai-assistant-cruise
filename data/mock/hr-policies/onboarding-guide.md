# NCL New Hire Onboarding Guide

**Welcome to Norwegian Cruise Line!**

This guide covers your first week with NCL. Complete each step and check it off in the HR Hub onboarding checklist.

## Before Your First Day

- Confirm your start date and reporting location with HR
- Ensure passport and medical certificate are valid (minimum 6 months validity)
- Complete pre-boarding forms sent to your email
- Pack per the uniform and personal items list for your department

## Day 1 — Arrival & Orientation

### Morning
1. **Report to Crew Services** (Deck 2, Port side) by 08:00
2. Present passport, medical certificate, and signed contract
3. Collect your **ID badge** and cabin assignment
4. Complete biometric registration for ship access

### Afternoon
5. Attend **Welcome Orientation** (Theater, Deck 6) at 14:00
   - Company history and values
   - Safety culture overview
   - Guest service standards
6. Meet your **Department Head** — introduction and team briefing
7. Tour your work area and review shift schedule

## Day 2 — Safety & Compliance

8. **Mandatory Safety Training** (Training Room, Deck 4)
   - Fire safety and extinguisher use
   - Emergency procedures and muster stations
   - Personal protective equipment
9. Complete **USPH sanitation briefing** (Food & Beverage staff) or general sanitation awareness (all staff)
10. Review department-specific safety procedures

## Day 3 — Systems & Processes

11. **IT Setup:** Email, crew portal, timekeeping system
    - IT Help Desk: Deck 3, or email it-help@ncl.crew
12. **Payroll & Benefits Review** with HR representative
    - Confirm bank details for salary deposit
    - Review benefit elections
13. Complete department-specific training modules in SAP SuccessFactors Learning

## Day 4–5 — Department Integration

14. Shadow experienced team member
15. Complete role-specific certification requirements
16. First independent shift (with supervisor oversight)

## First Week Checklist Summary

| Step | Task | Responsible |
|------|------|-------------|
| 1 | Welcome orientation | HR / Training |
| 2 | Submit documents | You + Crew Services |
| 3 | Collect uniform & ID | Crew Services |
| 4 | Safety training | Safety Officer |
| 5 | IT account setup | IT Help Desk |
| 6 | Meet department head | Your Manager |
| 7 | Payroll & benefits review | HR |

## Need Help?

- **HR Hub** (this portal): ask any HR question 24/7
- **Crew Services:** Deck 2, open 07:00–19:00
- **HR Email:** hr@ncl.com
- **Emergency:** Dial 911 from any ship phone

Welcome aboard! We're glad to have you on the NCL team.
