# NCL Leave Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Applies to:** All NCL employees — ship, hotel, and shore-based staff

## Annual Leave Entitlement

| Tenure | Days per Year |
|--------|---------------|
| 0–2 years | 20 days |
| 2–5 years | 25 days |
| 5+ years | 30 days |

Contract employees on board follow the MLC (Maritime Labour Convention) minimum of 2.5 days per month of service, which may exceed the standard entitlement.

## Requesting Leave

1. Submit leave request via the NCL Crew Portal at least **30 days in advance** for planned leave
2. For ship-based staff: requests must be approved by Department Head and Staff Captain
3. For shore-based staff: requests require direct manager approval
4. Emergency leave: contact your manager immediately; documentation required within 48 hours

## Leave Types

- **Annual Leave:** Paid time off per entitlement above
- **Sick Leave:** Up to 10 days per year with medical certificate after 3 consecutive days
- **Compassionate Leave:** Up to 5 days for immediate family bereavement
- **Maternity/Paternity:** Per local law and MLC requirements (minimum 14 weeks maternity for seafarers)

## Blackout Periods

Leave requests may be restricted during:
- Peak season (June–August for Alaska, December–February for Caribbean)
- Dry dock periods (as communicated by Fleet Operations)
- USPH inspection windows (72 hours before and after inspection)

## Carryover

Unused annual leave may be carried over up to **5 days** into the next calendar year. Remaining balance is forfeited unless exceptional circumstances are approved by HR Director.
