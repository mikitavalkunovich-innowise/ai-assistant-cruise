# NCL Code of Conduct

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Applies to:** All NCL employees, contractors, and agency staff

## Our Values

Norwegian Cruise Line is committed to providing exceptional guest experiences while maintaining the highest standards of integrity, safety, and respect for all.

## Professional Standards

All employees must:
- Treat guests, colleagues, and partners with dignity and respect
- Maintain professional appearance per department grooming standards
- Report to work fit for duty — zero tolerance for alcohol or drug impairment on duty
- Protect guest privacy and company confidential information
- Follow all safety procedures without exception

## Anti-Harassment Policy

NCL maintains a zero-tolerance policy for harassment, discrimination, and bullying based on race, gender, religion, nationality, sexual orientation, disability, or any protected characteristic.

**Reporting:** Report incidents to your manager, HR, or the confidential Ethics Hotline: +1-800-NCL-ETHICS (available 24/7, anonymous option available).

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any financial interest, relationship, or activity that could influence business decisions. Gifts from vendors exceeding $50 USD must be reported to Compliance.

## Social Media

- Do not post guest photos or videos without explicit consent
- Do not represent personal opinions as company positions
- Do not share proprietary information, itineraries, or security procedures

## Disciplinary Process

1. Verbal warning (documented)
2. Written warning
3. Final written warning
4. Termination

Serious violations (theft, violence, safety breaches, harassment) may result in immediate termination.

## Whistleblower Protection

Employees who report violations in good faith are protected from retaliation per company policy and applicable law.
