# IMO STCW Convention — Certification Requirements

**Source:** International Maritime Organization — Standards of Training, Certification and Watchkeeping  
**Applies to:** All seafarers on NCL vessels

## Overview

The STCW Convention (as amended in 2010 Manila Amendments) sets minimum qualification standards for seafarers worldwide. NCL requires all deck, engine, and hotel staff on board to hold applicable STCW certifications.

## Basic Safety Training (BST) — Required for All Seafarers

Every seafarer must complete Basic Safety Training before assignment to shipboard duties:

1. **Personal Survival Techniques** — 8 hours
2. **Fire Prevention and Fire Fighting** — 16 hours
3. **Elementary First Aid** — 8 hours
4. **Personal Safety and Social Responsibilities** — 4 hours

**Validity:** 5 years from date of completion. Refresher training required before expiry.

## Advanced Certifications

### Advanced Fire Fighting (AFF)
- Required for: Safety officers, fire team leaders, designated crew
- Duration: 24 hours initial; 8 hours refresher
- Validity: 5 years

### Medical First Aid
- Required for: At least one crew member per department
- Duration: 24 hours initial; refresher per flag state requirements
- Validity: 5 years

### Crowd Management Training
- Required for: All crew with guest contact responsibilities
- Duration: 8 hours
- Validity: 5 years

## Record Keeping

Per STCW Regulation I/2, companies must:
- Maintain records of all crew certifications
- Verify certificates before embarkation
- Report expired certifications to flag state authority
- Ensure no seafarer serves without valid required certification

## NCL Internal Requirements

Beyond STCW minimums, NCL requires:
- Pre-embarkation certification verification via SAP SuccessFactors Learning
- Manager notification 14 days before certification expiry
- Employee personal reminder at 14 days before expiry
- No embarkation permitted with expired mandatory certifications

## Flag State Requirements

NCL vessels operate under the Bahamas flag. Additional Bahamas Maritime Authority requirements may apply. Consult Fleet Compliance for flag-specific requirements.

## Penalties for Non-Compliance

- Port State Control detention risk
- USCG inspection deficiencies
- Internal disciplinary action up to and including removal from vessel
- Company liability for operating with uncertified crew
