# USCG Maritime Compliance — Inspection & Reporting Requirements

**Source:** US Coast Guard NVIC guidance and 46 CFR regulations  
**Applies to:** Foreign-flag cruise vessels calling at US ports

## Port State Control Inspections

USCG conducts Port State Control (PSC) examinations of foreign-flag vessels in US waters. Cruise ships are designated **Priority I** vessels subject to expanded examinations.

### Common Deficiency Areas
1. **Fire safety systems** — detection, suppression, emergency equipment
2. **Life-saving appliances** — lifeboats, rafts, muster procedures
3. **Crew certifications** — STCW compliance, medical fitness
4. **Safety management** — SMS implementation, drill records
5. **Pollution prevention** — MARPOL compliance, oil record book

### Deficiency Codes
- **Code 30:** Detainable deficiency — vessel may be detained
- **Code 17:** Rectify before departure
- **Code 10:** Rectify at next port
- **Code 46:** Rectify within 14 days

## Mandatory Reporting

### Notice of Arrival (NOA)
- Submit via NVMC at least **96 hours** before arrival at US port
- Include: crew list, passenger count, last port of call, cargo declaration
- Updates required for changes in ETA > 6 hours

### Maritime Casualty Reporting
Report to USCG within **5 days** for:
- Grounding, collision, or allision
- Loss of main propulsion or steering
- Fire or flooding
- Loss of life or serious injury
- Pollution incidents (immediate notification required)

### Crew List Requirements
- Submit crew list 96 hours before arrival
- Include: name, nationality, date of birth, position, certificate details
- STCW certificate numbers must be verifiable

## Certificate of Compliance (COC)

Foreign passenger vessels must hold a valid USCG Certificate of Compliance:
- Initial examination required before first US port call
- Annual examination thereafter
- COC covers: structural fire protection, lifesaving, navigation safety, pollution prevention

## Drug & Alcohol Testing

Per 46 CFR Part 16:
- Pre-employment testing required
- Random testing: minimum 50% of crew annually for drugs, 25% for alcohol
- Post-incident testing mandatory
- Records maintained for 5 years

## NCL Compliance Actions

- Designated USCG liaison officer on each vessel
- Pre-arrival checklist completed 72 hours before US port call
- All deficiencies from previous PSC tracked in compliance system until closed
- Quarterly USCG compliance briefing for all ship management
