# Sample Employment Contract (Demo)

## Parties
- **Employer:** Atlantic Services LLC
- **Employee:** [Employee Name]

## 1. Term of Employment
Employment begins on January 15, 2024 and continues until terminated in accordance with Section 8.

## 2. Position and Duties
Employee shall serve as Senior Operations Manager and perform duties as assigned by the Employer.

## 3. Compensation
Employee shall receive a base salary of $85,000 per year, payable bi-weekly.

## 4. Non-Compete Clause
For a period of **24 months** following termination of employment for any reason, Employee shall not directly or indirectly engage in any business that competes with Employer within a **100-mile radius** of any Employer office.

## 5. Confidentiality
Employee agrees to maintain confidentiality of all proprietary information indefinitely.

## 6. Termination
**6.1** Employer may terminate employment **at any time, with or without cause**, upon **14 days written notice**.

**6.2** Employee may terminate employment upon 30 days written notice.

**6.3** Upon termination, Employee shall immediately return all company property.

## 7. Severance
No severance pay is provided unless required by applicable law.

## 8. Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.

## 9. Dispute Resolution
All disputes shall be resolved through **binding arbitration** in New York, NY. Employee waives the right to a jury trial.

## 10. Entire Agreement
This document constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.

---
*Signed: Atlantic Services LLC / Employee*
