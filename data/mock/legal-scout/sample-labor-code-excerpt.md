# Sample Labor Code Excerpts (Offline Demo)

## Article 77 — Notice of Termination

An employer must provide written notice of termination at least two weeks in advance unless the employee has committed a serious breach of duty. Termination without proper notice may entitle the employee to compensation equal to average earnings for the notice period.

## Article 81 — Grounds for Dismissal

Dismissal at the initiative of the employer is permitted only on grounds expressly listed in this Code, including: liquidation of the organization, staff reduction, failure to meet job requirements after a warning, and gross violation of labor duties. Dismissal without valid grounds may be challenged in court.

## Article 178 — Severance Pay

Upon dismissal due to staff reduction or liquidation, the employer shall pay severance of at least one average monthly wage. Additional payments may be required under collective agreements or employment contracts, but only if they exceed statutory minimums.

## Article 37 — Working Hours and Overtime

The normal working week shall not exceed 40 hours. Overtime work requires written consent of the employee except in emergencies. Overtime shall be compensated at not less than one and one-half times the regular rate.

## Article 56 — Non-Compete Restrictions

Non-compete clauses in employment contracts are enforceable only if they are reasonable in scope, duration, and geographic area, and if the employee receives adequate compensation during the restricted period. Clauses exceeding 12 months or lacking compensation may be deemed unenforceable.
